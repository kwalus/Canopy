Pilot takeaway

An ILS is not won at minimums. It is won by building a clean intercept, configuring early, and being willing to go missed before the approach starts to unravel.

  • Track the needles with small, boring corrections.
  • Brief the missed approach before you need it.
  • Do not negotiate with an unstable gate.
