Observation

Acidic region

Large pH changes require substantial titrant before the jump. The flask remains strongly acidic.

Technique

Approach the endpoint slowly

Once you are within about 1 mL of equivalence, switch to dropwise additions and swirl continuously.

Reasoning

Stoichiometry first

Before equivalence, pH comes from excess H⁺. After equivalence, pH comes from excess OH⁻.
